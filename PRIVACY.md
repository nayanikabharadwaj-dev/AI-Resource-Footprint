# Privacy policy — Nayanika's AI Resource Footprint Extension

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This extension estimates the electricity and water used by your conversations on claude.ai.

**What it reads.** On claude.ai pages only, it reads the text of your messages and Claude's replies so it can count characters and estimate length. It does not read any other website.

**What it stores.** Only running totals: an estimated token count and the number of exchanges for your current and most recent session. These are kept in your browser's local extension storage. Message text is never stored.

**What it shares.** Nothing. All calculations happen in your browser. The extension makes no network requests, has no analytics, and sends no data to the developer or any third party.

**Removing data.** Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.

The extension is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
